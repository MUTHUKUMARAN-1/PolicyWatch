Unified Policy Tracking Platform

Welcome to PolicyWatch. This user portal integrates the project's three core engineering modules into one interface.

🗄️

Database Management System SQLite3 Core

Structured relational schema (tracker.db) with 5 normalized tables: Users, Websites, PrivacyPolicies, TermsConditions, and ChangeHistory. Supports foreign keys, password hashing, and cascade deletions.

🔍

Semantic Change Detection NLP Classifier

Analyzes policy revisions at the sentence level. Automatically classifies clauses such as Data Retention, Third-Party Sharing, Arbitration Waivers, and Biometrics while filtering whitespace and cookie banner noise.

🌐

Web Scraping & Versioning ptct-scraper

Monitors live and synthetic Acme test sources. Tracks timestamps, content hashes, selector shifts, and generates verifiable snapshot evidence across refresh cycles.
